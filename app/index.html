Field guide

Each box occupies its place in the periodic table. Across a row, atomic number increases. Within a group, elements tend to share chemical properties.

Read the lattice

Color identifies the chemical family. Box depth increases with the square root of atomic number. The two detached rows belong to periods 6 and 7. Enable “Show theoretical” to explore proposed elements 119 and 120.

Navigate

Drag to orbit. Use + and − to zoom. Hover or use the arrow keys on the lattice to inspect. Click a box or press Enter to travel inside. Press / to search and Escape to return.

Inside an element

The Bohr diagram groups electrons by shell. Orbits, nucleus size and particle spacing are schematic. Unknown values and predicted properties are labeled in the record.
